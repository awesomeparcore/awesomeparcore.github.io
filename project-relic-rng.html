Relic RNG

A browser idle game: roll for relics with posted odds, dig down through the earth, and beat the boss at the bottom of each layer.

UnityHTML5Idle Game

GAME_PAGE.TXT

# overview

Every roll pays cash, cash buys upgrades, and upgrades let you dig deeper and roll rarer relics. It runs in the browser, is free to play, and saves your progress automatically. It's an early version, and more layers are on the way.

# how it plays

  • Roll for relics. Every relic shows its real odds, from 1 in 2 up to 1 in 1 billion. Luck makes rare relics more likely, and you can turn your luck down to farm the common ones. Duplicate copies power up each relic's bonus, and every new discovery gives permanent luck.
  • Dig through the layers. Dirt (0–100 m) has a Fossil Dig minigame and the Mole King. Stone (100–300 m) has a Mining minigame where you time your swings, and the Rock Golem. Each layer has its own relics, fossils, titles and upgrades.
  • Beat the bosses. Bosses bounce around the arena while you click to hit them, each with its own attack pattern. You dodge the red zones with your cursor before they strike, and every win counts toward permanent milestone rewards.
  • Rebirth and go deeper. Beating a boss lets you rebirth for a bigger cash boost and the next layer. Rebirth upgrades, fossils, titles and achievements carry over, so each run goes faster than the last.

# features

  • 20 relics, 12 fossils and 28 titles to collect, plus a shiny variant that doubles a relic's bonus once you reach 200 m.
  • Auto-roll and offline progress, so the game keeps earning while you're away.
  • A short tutorial for new players.
  • Played entirely with the mouse (Space also swings in Mining). Progress saves in the browser, so clearing site data resets it.
